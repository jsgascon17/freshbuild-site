Who I Am

Hey, I'm Joseph Gascon

I'm a web developer based in Rochester, NY who believes every small business deserves a website that makes a great first impression—without breaking the bank.

I started FreshBuild because I saw too many local businesses stuck with outdated, hard-to-use websites. Big agencies charge thousands for basic sites. I offer the same quality at prices small businesses can actually afford.

Whether your site was built 5 years ago or 15, I can help bring it into the present—with clean design, smooth functionality, and a look that builds trust with your customers.

The Process

How It Works

Getting a website built is simple. Here's what to expect.

1

Get in Touch

Contact me through the website or email. Share basic info about your business and what you're looking for.

2

Discuss Your Project

We'll chat about your goals, timeline, and budget. I'll recommend the right package and provide a clear quote.

3

Provide Your Content

Send your business info, text, logo, and photos. Don't have everything ready? I'll guide you through it.

4

Design & Build

I design and build your website, keeping you updated along the way. You'll see progress throughout.

5

Review & Revise

Review your site and request changes. Revisions are included—I tweak until you love it.

6

Launch

Once approved, we launch! I'll help set up hosting and make sure everything works perfectly.
