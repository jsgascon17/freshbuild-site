## Key Design Patterns in Finance

### Colors
- Almost all use **navy blue** as primary
- White backgrounds with lots of whitespace
- Subtle accent colors (light blue, orange, turquoise)
- NO bright/flashy colors

### Logos
- Simple wordmarks or minimal geometric marks
- Nothing complicated or clip-art-y
- Usually just company name in clean typography

### Layout
- Clean, grid-based, lots of whitespace
- Featured transactions prominently displayed
- Professional team headshots
- Minimal animation, no gimmicks

## Recommendations for Peter

### Logo Direction
1. **Refined current** - Keep triangle motif but make more sophisticated, deeper navy
2. **Monogram** - "NCP" mark, more premium feel
3. **Abstract growth** - Upward momentum symbol
4. **Clean wordmark** - Just refined typography, no icon

### Color Palette Suggestions
- Primary: Deep navy (#002855 or similar)
- Secondary: White, light grays
- Accent: Subtle blue or gold (not bright yellow)

### Website Improvements
- Lots of whitespace
- Prominent transactions section
- Professional imagery (no clip art)
- Clean typography
- Mobile-responsive (current Wix is, but custom would be better)
