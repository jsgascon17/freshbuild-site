### Monetization Paths Discussed

**1. UEFN / Fortnite Creative (Recommended to start)**
- Build maps/experiences in Fortnite
- Epic pays based on player engagement
- Fastest path to revenue
- Uses "Verse" scripting language

**2. Unreal Marketplace Assets**
- Sell 3D models, blueprints, materials
- 88% revenue to creator
- Need Blender skills first
- Must be ORIGINAL content (no Fortnite IP)

**3. Full Game Development**
- Make and sell games on Steam/Epic Store
- Longest timeline (6-24 months)
- Highest potential but most competitive
