What Canandaigua Businesses Are Up Against

Canandaigua sits at the north end of Canandaigua Lake, and that geography drives the economy. Tourism, the lakefront, the wine trail and seasonal visitors shape a business calendar that looks nothing like a year-round suburb's.

Seasonality changes what a website has to handle. Hours change. Menus change. Something is closed in February that is packed in July. A site that cannot be updated quickly becomes wrong for half the year, and a wrong hours listing costs a visit.

Canandaigua businesses also serve two audiences at once: locals who know them, and visitors deciding from a phone in a hotel or a car. Those two need different things from the same page.
