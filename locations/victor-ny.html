What Victor Businesses Are Up Against

Victor has been one of the fastest-growing towns in Ontario County, and Eastview Mall plus the Route 96 corridor pull shoppers from well beyond the town line.

That creates an unusual split. Businesses near the mall compete for regional traffic — people who could just as easily drive to Pittsford or Canandaigua. Businesses in Victor Village and out toward Fishers serve a local base that mostly finds them by search or word of mouth.

A Victor website usually has to do both jobs at once: convince someone deciding between towns, and make it effortless for a neighbour who already knows you to book.
