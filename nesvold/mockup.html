Who We Are

A Different Kind of Partner

Industry Specialists with Patient Capital

Nesvold Capital Partners is a merchant bank that advises and makes supportive, non-control investments in growth-oriented financial services firms.

We focus on companies with innovative offerings and high-quality management teams in asset and wealth management, securities, fintech, and related businesses.

Unlike traditional private equity, we deploy long-life, family office capital with no predetermined time horizon for exit — allowing us to be true long-term partners in your success.
