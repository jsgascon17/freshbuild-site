Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

FreshBuild offers affordable website packages starting at $299 for a landing page, $299 for a single-page website, and $999 for a multi-page website with up to 8 pages. Custom projects are quoted based on scope.

How long does it take to build a website?

Turnaround times depend on the project: Landing pages take about 1 week, single-page websites take 1-2 weeks, and multi-page websites take 2-4 weeks. Custom projects vary based on complexity.

What do I need to provide for my website?

You'll need to provide your business information, any text content you want included, your logo (if you have one), and any photos or images. If you don't have content ready, I can help guide you through what's needed.

Do you offer website hosting?

I don't provide hosting directly, but I'll help you set up affordable hosting. Most small business websites can be hosted for $3-10 per month through providers like Netlify, Vercel, or traditional hosts.

Can you update my existing website?

Yes! I offer hourly support at $20/hour for small updates, content changes, and bug fixes. For larger updates or redesigns, we can discuss a package that fits your needs.

What's the difference between a landing page and single-page website?

A landing page ($299) is a single, focused page designed for one purpose like promoting a product or capturing leads. A single-page website ($299) is a complete scrolling website with multiple sections like About, Services, and Contact.

Do you build e-commerce websites?

Yes, e-commerce websites fall under custom projects and are quoted based on your specific needs - number of products, payment processing, shipping requirements, etc. Contact me for a custom quote.

What areas do you serve?

FreshBuild is based in Rochester, NY and primarily serves small businesses in Upstate New York including Rochester, Buffalo, and Syracuse. However, since web design is done remotely, I can work with clients anywhere.
